# Talktive Platform Features & Benefits

This document outlines the features available in the Talktive "Apartment Building" and distinguishes between free community features and **Talktive Plus** premium benefits.

---

## 🏛️ Free Community Features (For All Residents)

Talktive is built on the principle of open community and expressive personality. The following features are available to all residents who have passed through the lobby:

### 1. Unified Expression
- **Resident Name & Mood**: Every persona can have a custom name and mood emoji.
- **Interest Badges**: Residents can display up to 10 interests on their profile to help neighbors find them.
- **Platform-Wide Image Sharing**:
    - **Area**: Plaza, Lounges, Private Chats, and Moments.
    - **Requirement**: Floor 2+ is required to share images in **Public/Community Spaces** (Plaza and Lounges) to prevent spam.
    - **Private Chats**: Sharing images is always unrestricted by level in 1-on-1 conversations.

### 2. Global Communication
- **The Plaza**: Access to the building's heart and the "Global Lounge."
- **Lounge Discovery**: Search for and join any public community clubhouse.
- **Private Chats**: Initiate and maintain private conversations (subject to "Doorbell" safety policy).

### 3. Social Interaction
- **Vouching (Likes)**: Residents can vouch for neighbors to increase their Trust Score.
- **Commenting**: Interactive dialogue on Moments shared on the Bulletin Board.
- **Reporting**: Tools to help maintain a safe apartment environment.

### 4. Progress & Gamification
- **XP & Leveling (Floors)**: Earn XP through daily activity to climb the building's floors.
- **Streaks**: Visual indicators of your daily commitment to the community.
- **Daily Rewards**: Claim XP bonuses every 24 hours.
- **Achievements**: Unlock and display badges for platform milestones.

---

## 💎 Talktive Plus (Premium Benefits)

**Talktive Plus** is for residents who want to truly personalize their unit and enjoy specialized building utilities.

### 1. Premium Identity
- **Custom Avatars**: Upload your own image or design to use as your persona's avatar.
- **Exclusive Badges**: Special visual indicators of your Plus membership.

### 2. Advanced Communication
- **Voice Messages**: Record and send audio messages in any chat thread (Plaza, Lounges, or Private).
- **Read Receipts**: See when your neighbors have read your messages (optional).
- **Typing Indicators**: Show others when you are crafting a message (optional).

### 3. Resident Discovery & Social Management
- **Neighbors Discovery**: Access to the "People Search" screen to find and "Knock" on specific residents' doors.
- **Online Indicator Tuning**: Toggle your online status visibility to stay undercover or be the life of the party.
- **Chat Persistence**: Option to preserve private chats from the 30-day ephemerality cleanup.

### 4. Ad-Free Living
- **No Ads**: A completely seamless experience without interstitial ads during navigation.

---

## 🛡️ Feature Gating Philosophy

Talktive uses a **Security First** approach to feature access:

- **The Floor System**: Certain features (e.g., posting Moments or sharing media in public) are gated by **Floor Level** rather than payment. This ensures that only residents who have integrated into the community can impact the public environment.
- **Privacy as a Right**: Privacy settings (Blocking, Reporting, Hiding from Discovery) are **always free** and accessible to all residents, irrespective of their subscription status.
